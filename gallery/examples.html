04 / DROP IT INTO YOUR PROJECT

Your next build.

Serve the bundle beside your app.
Keep your backend. Share the interface.

CSS first.
Enhance when you need it.

Add .b5-ui to your application wrapper. Load the stylesheet, then opt into interactive tabs, dialogs and copy actions.

The same files can be embedded in Rust, served by Python, or imported by a browser application. Asset URLs resolve relative to the stylesheet.
